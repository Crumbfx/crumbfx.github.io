Crumb Uploader

Send your finished video to your TikTok inbox without moving the file to your phone. Open TikTok afterwards to add a caption and post it yourself.

  1. 1. Connect TikTok

    Log in with TikTok once. Crumb only sees your display name and avatar, and can only put drafts in your inbox.

  2. 2. Choose your video

    MP4, MOV or WebM. It stays on your device until you press send.

  3. 3. Send to TikTok

What it asks TikTok for

  • user.info.basic: to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to place your video in your inbox as a draft.

Crumb never posts for you and does not use Direct Post. Free, no payment, no ads.
