Crumb Uploader

Finish an edit, prepare it for TikTok, and send it to your TikTok inbox without moving the file to your phone.

How it works

  1. Choose your finished video in the browser. It is prepared on your own device.
  2. Connect your TikTok account once with TikTok Login Kit. Crumb only sees your TikTok display name and avatar.
  3. Press Send to TikTok. The video goes to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. Open the TikTok app, add your caption and post it yourself. Crumb never posts for you.

What it asks TikTok for

  • user.info.basic: to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to place your video in your inbox as a draft.

Free

Crumb Uploader is free to use. No payment and no ads. You can disconnect TikTok at any time on the same screen.
