Crumb Uploader

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026.

What Crumb Uploader is

Crumb Uploader is a free tool by the Crumb team that prepares a video you choose and sends it to your own TikTok inbox.

Your video

Your video is read and prepared in your browser. When you press Send to TikTok it is sent to TikTok to be placed in your inbox as a draft. Crumb does not keep a copy of your video or audio. If the upload passes through Crumb's server, it is relayed in parts and not stored.

What we get from TikTok

When you connect TikTok through TikTok Login Kit, we receive your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), and permission to upload drafts to your inbox (scope video.upload). We do not read your videos, messages, followers or any other account data, and we never post for you.

What we store

We store your TikTok open ID, display name, avatar link and the access tokens TikTok gives us, encrypted on our server, so you stay connected. We also store a short record of each upload (file name, size and time) so you can see your history.

How we use it

Only to show which account is connected and to send your video to your inbox when you ask. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it for ads.

Disconnecting and deleting

Press Disconnect on the Send to TikTok card at any time. This revokes the connection with TikTok and deletes the tokens we stored. You can also remove Crumb Uploader in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. To have your remaining records deleted, message us on the Crumb Discord server and we will remove them.

Children

Crumb Uploader is not for people under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Questions: ask in the Crumb Discord server.
